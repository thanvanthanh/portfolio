Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026
Last updated: 3 November 2026

Zeitlohn records working time, calculates overtime, and estimates gross earnings. This policy explains how personal information is handled in the Zeitlohn mobile app, including for users in the European Economic Area (EEA), particularly Germany.

Your work records stay on your device. Zeitlohn has no developer-operated backend. It uses Firebase Authentication, Firebase Remote Config, and Google AdMob as separate online services for sign-in, app configuration, and advertising. These services process account or technical data as described below.

2. Data stored on your device

Work dates, times, breaks, notes, wage rates, overtime rules, and preferences are stored on your device, where the app performs calculations and generates reports. Work records are not uploaded to Firebase or synchronized with a server. Signing in does not upload, synchronize, or back up your work records. We cannot access or recover these local records remotely. Your operating system may back up app data separately, depending on your device settings.

PDF, DOCX, and CSV exports are generated on your device. Sharing or saving them through another service sends copies to your chosen recipients or service, whose privacy practices apply. Avoid unnecessary personal or sensitive information in notes.

Where applicable, requested features rely on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Necessary device access relies on Section 25(2) TDDDG in Germany; optional access requires consent where applicable.

3. Online services and advertising choices

Optional accounts: Firebase Authentication processes email, account identifiers, sign-in provider, verification status, credentials, and technical information such as IP addresses. Google or Apple sign-in may supply a name or relay email. Required authentication details enable account access under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR; security and abuse prevention rely on Article 6(1)(f). Firebase manages the online account separately from your local work records. Passwords are not stored in the local work-record database.

Configuration: Firebase Remote Config uses installation identifiers to retrieve settings, including whether advertising is enabled, without uploading work records.

Advertising: Google AdMob and advertising partners may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, approximate location, app/device information, ad interactions, and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and detect fraud. The app does not send your work records, wage rates, or notes to AdMob or its advertising partners. Non-personalized ads can still process personal data.

Advertising device access and related processing requiring consent must wait for permission under Section 25(1) TDDDG and Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Device advertising settings and iOS permissions provide additional controls.

Manage consent: Where available, open Settings → Privacy → Privacy choices to review your advertising preferences and change or withdraw your consent. You can also decline consent when the consent form is first displayed.

Details: Firebase privacy information, Google Privacy Policy, Google partner-app information, and Apple Privacy Policy.

Contact us for copies of applicable safeguards, with confidential details redacted. Disclosures required by law or necessary for legal claims rely on Article 6(1)(c) or (f) GDPR.

5. Retention and deletion

Local records and preferences remain until deleted or reset through the app or device controls. Manage backups and exported copies separately. Delete your account in the app's Account section or contact us; verification may be required. Account deletion and local-record deletion are separate actions. Deleting your Firebase account does not erase local work records; deleting local work records does not delete your Firebase account.

Firebase Authentication retains account data until deletion is initiated. Firebase states that authentication IP addresses remain for a few weeks and other authentication data is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days after deletion begins; see Firebase retention information.

  • Remote Config installation data: Firebase retains installation IDs until the customer calls the deletion API; it then removes the data from live and backup systems within 180 days. See Firebase retention information. Contact our privacy email for assistance with installation-data deletion. Deleting an authentication account is separate from deleting a Firebase installation ID.
  • Advertising data and consent records: Google retains advertising data according to its purpose and the criteria in its retention policy, including providing the service, security, fraud prevention, and legal obligations. Consent choices can be reviewed or withdrawn through the app privacy options described above.
  • Support correspondence: We retain support emails while needed to resolve your request, handle related follow-up, or meet applicable legal obligations; we delete them when no longer needed. Gmail automatically permanently deletes messages from Trash after 30 days unless removed sooner; see Gmail deletion information.

Legal duties or specific legal claims may require limited longer retention. We use reasonable security measures, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

6. Your rights and policy changes

Under applicable GDPR conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, or portability and withdraw consent. You may object to legitimate-interest processing for reasons relating to your situation, and to direct marketing at any time.

Contact the privacy email above. We normally respond within one month and explain any permitted extension within that month. Proportionate identity verification may be needed. We can guide you to controls for device-only records.

You may complain to a supervisory authority where you live, work, or believe an infringement occurred, without contacting us first. See the German authorities directory.

We do not use earnings calculations for automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Zeitlohn is not directed at children; contact us about suspected collection of a child's data.

We will post policy changes here and update the date, providing additional notice or obtaining consent where required.
